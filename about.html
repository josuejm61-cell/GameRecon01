About This Project

Retro Game Archive was created as a digital documentation project focused on preserving the history of video games. The goal is to provide a structured overview of key eras, iconic titles, and major shifts in gaming technology.

Why Retro Games Matter

Classic games laid the foundation for modern game design. Mechanics, storytelling, and innovation from earlier decades continue to influence today’s industry.

Project Scope

This website highlights historically significant titles and eras. It does not provide downloadable content or online gameplay. Instead, it functions as an informational archive.
