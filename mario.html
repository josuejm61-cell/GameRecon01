Super Mario Bros (1985)

Released in 1985 for the Nintendo Entertainment System, Super Mario Bros became one of the most influential platform games in history.

It introduced side-scrolling mechanics, power-ups like the Super Mushroom, and world-based level progression that became standard in future games.

Its success helped revive the video game industry after the 1983 crash.
