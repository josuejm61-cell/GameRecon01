About This Project

Retro Game Archive was created as a digital documentation project focused on preserving the history of video games. The goal is to provide a a short overview of key eras, iconic titles, and how gaming evolved over time. Project Scope

This website highlights historically significant titles and eras. It does not provide downloadable content or online gameplay. Instead, it functions as an informational archive.
